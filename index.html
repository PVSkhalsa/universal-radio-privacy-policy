Universal Radio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026
App: Universal Radio (Android package com.pvskhalsa.universalradio)
Developer: PVS Khalsa

Universal Radio plays live internet radio stations. This page explains what the app does with information.

Summary

  • The developer does not collect, receive or store any personal data from the app.
  • There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking code.
  • When you play a station, your phone connects directly to that station's own server. The developer is not involved in that connection.
  • Your favorites, sleep timer choices and recordings stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

The app saves your favorite stations, your sleep timer settings and whether you have seen the welcome message. If you use the record button, the app saves the station's audio stream as a file in the app's own recordings folder on your phone. You can play, share and delete recordings in the app. Sharing opens Android's share sheet and sends a file only where you choose. Removing the app deletes this data, and so does the Android setting "Clear storage".

Android's backup feature may include this app's data according to your phone's own backup settings. The developer does not receive these backups.

Stations and streams

The station list is built into the app. It comes from the radio-browser.info station database, which is community maintained and states that its data is in the public domain. The app does not contact radio-browser.info while you use it.

When you play or record a station, your phone sends a request to that station's server. That server can see your phone's internet address and the app's name and version, as any server you connect to can. The station may keep logs and may have its own privacy policy. Some stations do not use a secure connection, so others on the same network may be able to see what you listen to. The developer does not control or see this. Streams belong to their stations; this app only plays them. A station can be offline, blocked or changed at any time.

Recording

Recording saves the station's own audio stream. It does not record your screen, microphone or other apps. Record only what you have the right to keep.

Permissions

The app uses these Android permissions: Internet and network state (to play streams), Foreground service for media playback and Wake lock (to keep audio playing while the screen is off), and Notifications (to show the playback controls). It does not use contacts, location, microphone, camera, SMS, call log or storage permissions.

Update checks

A version installed from Google Play asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, using Google's Play In-App Updates library. The Google Play Store app does that on your phone and Google handles that information under its own terms. The developer does not receive it. Versions not installed from Google Play do not get update prompts.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Station content is provided by third parties and is not reviewed by the developer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details on the app's Google Play listing.
